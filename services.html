Our Business

Three lines of business, delivered as one service by a single accountable team.

01

Business 01

Office Equipment

Equipment and furnishings that keep offices productive.

We source office equipment to the partner's specification, compare options and prices, and deliver items ready for use.

  • Office furniture
  • Printers & office machines
  • Computer peripherals
  • Filing & storage
02

Business 02

General Supplies

Consumables, printed materials and branded items.

From day-to-day consumables to printed cards, stickers, merchandise and team apparel, supplied on demand or on a regular schedule.

  • Stationery & consumables
  • Printing: cards & stickers
  • Corporate merchandise
  • Apparel & jerseys
03

Business 03

Logistics & Procurement

End-to-end sourcing and delivery on the partner's behalf.

We identify suppliers, obtain comparative quotations, purchase and deliver, keeping complete records for the partner's audit and reference. We also fulfil orders as a subcontractor.

  • Supplier sourcing
  • Comparative quotations
  • Purchasing
  • Delivery & subcontract fulfilment
