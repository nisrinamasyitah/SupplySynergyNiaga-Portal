Who We Are

One accountable partner for your organisation's supply needs.

Supply Synergy Niaga is a Malaysian supply company headquartered in Kedah. We support organisations with the equipment and supplies they rely on every day, and manage the sourcing and delivery behind them.

Instead of dealing with many suppliers, our partners work with a single team that quotes clearly, sources reliably and delivers on the agreed date, with proper documentation for every order.
