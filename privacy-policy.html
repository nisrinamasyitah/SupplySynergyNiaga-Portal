Introduction

Supply Synergy Niaga (Company No. 003892019-H) ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you provide to us voluntarily, including when you submit an enquiry form, request a quotation, apply for a job or contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Name, company name and position
  • Email address and phone number
  • Details of your enquiry, order or application
  • Resumes and supporting documents for job applications

How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations
  • To process, deliver and follow up on orders
  • To evaluate job and supplier applications
  • To communicate with you about our products and services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

Disclosure of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with parties who help us provide our services (such as suppliers, delivery partners and service providers), where required by law, or with your consent.

Enquiry forms on this website are delivered to us through a third-party form service (FormSubmit), which processes your submission in order to send it to our email.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you may request access to, or correction of, your personal data, and you may withdraw your consent to its use. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.
