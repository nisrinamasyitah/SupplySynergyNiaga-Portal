About Us

A Malaysian supply, logistics and procurement company built on integrity and reliability.

01Corporate Profile

One partner for what your organisation needs to operate.

Supply Synergy Niaga is a Malaysian supply company providing office equipment, general supplies, and logistics & procurement services to businesses, government agencies and institutions.

Instead of dealing with many suppliers, our clients work with a single team that quotes clearly, sources reliably and delivers on the agreed date, with proper documentation for every order. Our track record includes supply, printing and merchandise work for a federal agency, a municipal council and private brands.
